# Free100Leads

[Free100Leads](https://free100leads.com) is a B2B contact database platform giving sales, marketing, and business development teams daily access to verified business contacts, no signup and no card required. The database holds 147 million-plus contacts with job titles, companies, emails, and phone numbers.

## Claiming free leads

Every visitor can claim up to 100 free leads per day directly from the homepage, with the allocation resetting daily. There is no signup form and no credit card required.

## Filtering leads

Contacts can be filtered by country, industry, company size, job title, employee count, and email quality, which lets a sales team build a targeted outreach list instead of a random sample of the full database.

## Upgrading

Teams needing more than the free daily allocation can find paid subscription tiers at [/pricing](https://free100leads.com/pricing). Full feature details are at [/services](https://free100leads.com/services).

## API access

Developers can pull leads programmatically through the API documented at [/docs/api](https://free100leads.com/docs/api), useful for wiring the lead feed directly into a CRM or outreach tool.
